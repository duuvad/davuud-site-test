QuestionsAI workflow automation

What people ask about automation.

What is AI workflow automation?+

Using AI to carry out the repeatable steps of a team's work, such as turning an email into a brief, producing a document or checking a draft, to the business's own written rules, with a person approving what leaves the business.

Which workflows suit a professional services firm?+

The ones that repeat every week and depend on getting details right: capturing client requests, producing proposals and reports, checking work against house rules, and keeping track of what needs doing next.

Will it work with the tools we already use?+

Each workflow is designed around the tools the team already uses for email, documents and files. Mapping those tools is the first step of every project.

How do you measure whether it works?+

Each workflow is measured from the first week against a figure agreed at the start, such as response time to a brief, the share of work approved first time, or the mistakes caught before a client sees them.
