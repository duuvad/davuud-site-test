AI brand guidelines

Brand guidelines AI tools can follow.

Most brand guidelines were written for a designer or a writer who reads them and uses judgement. A growing share of proposals, decks and campaign variants is now drafted by AI tools, which follow rules literally and fill every gap with something generic. These are the parts a brand system needs once AI is producing some of the work, taken from the one Pivitt runs on.

What changesWhen AI drafts the work

Why the old guidelines stop working.

Judgement becomes instruction

A tool cannot read tone from a mood board. Voice has to be written as rules it can apply, with examples of right and wrong.

Every gap gets filled

Where the guidelines are silent, a tool writes something plausible and generic, so the gaps have to be closed in writing.

Figures and names drift

Without one approved source, numbers, client names and claims change a little every time they are retyped.

A complete setFive parts

What AI brand guidelines have to contain.

One

Voice, written as instructions

Sentence length, words to use and avoid, and how to open and close, with paired examples of right and wrong.

Two

An approved claims register

Every figure and client name with its exact wording and its source.

Three

Banned words and constructions

The words and sentence shapes the brand never uses, kept as a list a tool can check against.

Four

Names, terms and figures

How products, clients and numbers are written, the same way every time.

Five

A check on every draft

Every draft tested against the rules and marked ready, ready after fixes, or held.

QuestionsAI brand guidelines

What people ask about AI guidelines.

What are AI brand guidelines?+

Brand guidelines written so AI tools can follow them: voice as explicit rules with examples, an approved list of claims and figures, banned words and constructions, and a check that every draft passes before it is used.

How do they differ from ordinary brand guidelines?+

Ordinary guidelines rely on a person reading them and using judgement. AI brand guidelines turn that judgement into written rules and checks, because a tool applies what it is given literally and fills any gap with something generic.

Which AI tools can use them?+

Any tool that accepts written instructions or a rules file. At Pivitt the voice and the check are packaged so every tool the team uses drafts on brand and checks its own work.

Where do you start?+

With the brand's existing guidelines and a sample of recent work. The gaps show up quickly: the claims nobody approved, the words that keep recurring, and the places where the tone drifts.
