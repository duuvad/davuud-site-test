The challenge01 / 06

A flagship leaves no room for inconsistency

BMW Park Lane is one of the most prominent automotive retail operations in the UK, and it does not run like an ordinary dealership. It is a flagship, and it carries flagship standards. Three brands with their own audiences and their own design languages share one location and one marketing team, and each of them needs a steady flow of campaigns, launches and everyday communications.

The work is constant and the bar is set by the manufacturer. Every piece goes through BMW Group CI approval before it ships, and every piece has to read as part of the same business, whether it is a launch film for BMW, a social square for MINI or a wall in the Motorrad space.

How we worked02 / 06

An embedded partner inside the marketing operation

The relationship is built to run at the pace of the business. We sit inside the marketing operation as the team that turns a single idea into every format it needs to live in: a hero film, a social grid, print, environment and email. One campaign becomes hundreds of finished assets, each on brief and on brand, produced fast enough to keep three brands active at the same time.

The showroom, campaign, digital, film, print and social work is produced by the same team to the same standard. That continuity is what the model is for. The marketing team can move quickly because the system behind them is already in place, and the manufacturer's standard is met by people who have held it across all three brands for years.

The thinking03 / 06

One idea, carried in each brand's own voice

"Change is on the horizon" began as one strategic idea about a shifting market and a new way to buy. We ran it across BMW and MINI and treated each in its own language, so the same thought was told two ways. It was built as a film, a step guide, digital assets, print and in-store support, carried across eight stages in BMW's register and again in MINI's.

BMW carried it with restraint and scale. MINI took it into a brighter, more playful register for a younger audience. BMW Motorrad held its own world inside the showroom, built around the riding culture its customers live in. Keeping three brands distinct at one address matters as much as keeping each of them consistent, and the work is planned with both in view.

The work04 / 06

A new way to buy, and a steady voice when the doors closed

When buying behaviour moved online, Park Lane Direct gave customers a way to choose and take delivery of a new car or bike from home. We built the launch around eight clear steps, from first enquiry to handover, and produced the full set of materials that explained it: the film, the step guide, the digital assets and the in-store support. It was a change in how the business sold, made simple enough for a customer to act on.

When the showrooms closed, the harder task was tone. The brand had to stay present and reassuring without losing its standing. We built a communications set around a calm, forward-looking message and carried it through short film, a clear visual system, longform explainers and staff email signatures, so every contact with a customer said the same thing in the same voice.

The system05 / 06

From the full range to the smallest point of contact

As the line moved toward electric and hybrid, the product communications had to keep up. We built data-led range materials that present every model clearly, with specification, charging and pricing held in one consistent format across BMW and MINI. The template makes a complex, fast-moving range easy to read and quick to update.

Inside the flagship the brand is physical. We produce the environmental graphics, event design and showroom moments that carry the three brands through the space, from launch evenings to the everyday wayfinding that tells a customer where they are and what to expect.

A brand is judged at its smallest point of contact as much as its largest. The same care that goes into a launch film goes into a staff email signature, built across all three brands so that a message from anyone at Park Lane reads as the business itself.

The result06 / 06

Eight years, three brands, one consistent standard

Over eight years the partnership has delivered 160+ campaigns, each one recognisably part of the same business. The work runs at the pace of a flagship and holds the manufacturer's standard across BMW, MINI and BMW Motorrad.

The commercial effect sits with the marketing team. Because the system behind them is already in place, they can move quickly on a launch, a new service or a sudden change in trading conditions, and trust that what goes out will be on brand, CI approved and consistent with everything around it.
