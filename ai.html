QuestionsAI systems

What people ask before we build.

What is an AI system for a business?+

A set of tools and written rules that let AI do part of a team's work to the business's own standard: a record of what the business knows, the rules its work has to follow, checks on every draft, and the routines that keep all of it current.

What does building one involve?+

Three stages. Map where the team loses time and where the same mistake keeps happening. Build the rules and the system around the work the team already does. Run it on live work, measure it from day one and keep it current as the business changes.

What has it changed at Pivitt?+

A one-hour median response to a brief and 73% of tracked briefs approved first time, June to July 2026, 36 of 36 client conversations captured as briefs, and 15 mistakes caught before they reached a client in the system's first five days.

Can you build one for my business?+

Yes. Each system is rebuilt around your rules, your tools and the way your team works, and scoped and priced before work starts. The first step is a conversation about where your team loses time.
