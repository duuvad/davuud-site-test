Speaker bio

Davuud Ghani builds brands, and the AI systems behind them, for organisations that have outgrown the brand they started with. He founded Pivitt, the London brand consultancy, and built the AI systems it runs on: a company memory, a client portal, a brand system that checks every draft and a production pipeline. His work runs from strategy and positioning through to the proposals, decks and campaigns a business produces every week. His record includes 200,000+ assets produced across an 8-year BMW Group partnership, 160+ campaigns, and helping scale a start-up investment business past £40M in revenue in eighteen months. He works with professional services, property and investment firms on repositioning, bringing groups of businesses under one brand, and keeping a brand consistent once dozens of people are producing work in its name.
